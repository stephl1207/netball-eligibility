Rules used

Position groups

  • Central: C, WA, WD
  • Goal attacking: GA, GS
  • Goal defence: GD, GK

Dual signing: You cannot play the exact position you play for your Main / first team. A position group used in a Sub game is also unavailable for Second team play.

Subbing: You cannot sub in a position group already represented by a position you play for your Main or Second team, or in an exact position you have already subbed in.
